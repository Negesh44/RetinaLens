# Real fundus sample images: credits and licenses

All images come from Wikimedia Commons. Each was modified for the demo: cropped to the
retina, the area outside the circular field blacked out (removing panel labels and
captions), padded to a square, resized to at most 1024 px, and saved as JPEG.
Files marked "simulated" are further altered copies of the normal photo, made to test
the image-quality gate. Adapted versions of CC BY-SA images are shared under the same license.
